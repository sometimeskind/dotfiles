Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.

## Phase 1 — Structured critique

Play three roles in sequence, surfacing gaps without suggesting solutions:
1. **Skeptical senior developer** — what's underspecified or ambiguous?
2. **QA engineer** — what edge cases aren't covered?
3. **New team member** — what context is missing that isn't in the plan?

## Phase 2 — Resolve the gaps

Walk down each unresolved branch of the design tree, one question at a time. For each question:
- Provide your recommended answer
- If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore it instead of asking
